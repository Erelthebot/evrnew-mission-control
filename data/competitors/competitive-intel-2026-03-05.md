## 4. KEY INSIGHTS

### 🎯 Insight 1: The Digital Advertising Vacuum
**Attic Projects Company is the ONLY company running significant paid digital advertising in this market.** Despite 25+ competitors identified, the Facebook Ad Library shows exactly ONE local insulation company running ads in the Seattle/PNW region — Attic Projects, with 1,300+ ads running since February 2023.

This represents a **massive opportunity**: the digital advertising space is essentially wide open for Evrnew. Any investment in Google Ads or Facebook/Instagram ads will face minimal direct competition from other local insulators.

### 🎯 Insight 2: Attic Projects Company is the "Goliath" — But Has a Clear Playbook
Attic Projects runs a sophisticated, multi-campaign digital strategy:
- **Video-first content** (most ads are 16–61 second videos)
- **Problem-based hooks** (rodents, energy waste, drafts)
- **Seasonal messaging** (rodents in winter, energy savings year-round)
- **Multiple CTAs** (insulation, rodent proofing, general services)
- **Dual-domain strategy** (atticprojectsseattle.com for local trust, atticprojectscompany.com for corporate scale)
- **Premium positioning** with W2 employees, 10-year warranty, 2,200+ reviews

Their weakness: **premium pricing and corporate feel**. Local, family-owned messaging (which Evrnew has authentically) can counter this.

### 🎯 Insight 3: The "Pest Control + Insulation Bundle" Trend
Three competitors explicitly bundle pest/rodent control with insulation: Attic Projects, Eastside Exterminators, Super Attic Solutions, and Classic Pest Control. This is a proven upsell/cross-sell strategy that drives higher average ticket values. **Evrnew should consider partnerships or bundling language** around this pattern.

### 🎯 Insight 4: 10-Year Warranty as Standard in Premium Tier
Three of the strongest competitors (Attic Projects, Sound Crawls, The Insulation Company) all prominently feature 10-year warranties. **This has become table stakes for premium positioning.** If Evrnew doesn't offer or advertise a strong warranty, they'll appear weaker vs. these players.

### 🎯 Insight 5: Skagit County is the Least Contested Digital Territory
Of the three counties in Evrnew's service area, Skagit County has the fewest digitally active competitors:
- The Insulation Company (Bellingham) — NO paid ads
- PNW Eco Insulation (Burlington) — NO paid ads
- General Plus Insulation (Sedro Woolley) — NO paid ads
- Classic Pest Control (Mount Vernon) — NO paid ads

**This makes Skagit County the lowest-cost-per-click opportunity** for Google Ads targeting cities like Burlington, Mount Vernon, Anacortes, Oak Harbor, and even Bellingham.

### 🎯 Insight 6: Free Inspections are Universal — Differentiate the Post-Inspection Experience
Every major competitor offers free inspections. The differentiator is no longer the free inspection itself, but **how professional, visual, and comprehensive the post-inspection report is**. Attic Projects explicitly describes photos, videos, and itemized proposals. Evrnew's process should be equally (or more) visual and transparent.

### 🎯 Insight 7: Energy Rebates are an Underutilized Lead Hook
Multiple competitors mention energy rebates but don't emphasize them heavily. Sound Crawls has a button specifically labeled "Claim Special Energy Rebates." The Insulation Company includes rebate eligibility in proposals. **Washington state has active utility rebate programs** (PSE, Snohomish PUD, Puget Sound Energy) — this is an underutilized lead generation angle that Evrnew could own through content marketing.

### 🎯 Insight 8: North Snohomish and Skagit Are Fragmented
Cities like Marysville, Arlington, Monroe, Stanwood, and Snohomish (city) appear to be served primarily by smaller operators with no digital presence. **These mid-sized cities represent low-competition Google Ads territory** where Evrnew could likely own the search results with modest spend.

## 5. RECOMMENDED PRIORITY TARGETS TO OUTCOMPETE

### Priority 1: Own Snohomish County's Mid-Cities (Immediate)
**Cities:** Marysville, Arlington, Monroe, Snohomish, Stanwood, Lynnwood, Mukilteo, Edmonds, Bothell, Woodinville  
**Why:** Ecoshield (Marysville), Specialty Insulation NW (Arlington), and American Crawlspace Cleanouts (Lynnwood) are all passive, referral-dependent competitors with no digital advertising.  
**Play:** Launch Google Local Service Ads + Google Search Ads targeting "[city] insulation contractor" for each of these cities. Very low competition = low CPCs.

### Priority 2: Dominate Skagit County Digitally (First-Mover)
**Cities:** Burlington, Mount Vernon, Anacortes, Stanwood, Oak Harbor  
**Why:** ZERO confirmed paid digital advertisers in Skagit County for insulation. The Insulation Company (Bellingham) is the only established player and they don't run paid ads.  
**Play:** Become the first insulation company to run Google Ads and Facebook ads in Skagit County. Budget as little as $500–$1,000/month could dominate.

### Priority 3: Attack Attic Projects' Weakness (Ongoing)
**The Play:** Position Evrnew as the local, family-owned alternative to the corporate/multi-market Attic Projects.  
**Messaging:** "We live here. We only serve here. Your neighbors know us." vs. their "Do It Once / Do It Right" corporate polish.  
**Channels:** Facebook/Instagram ads with authentic before/after content, homeowner video testimonials, Google reviews campaign.

### Priority 4: Target Pest Control Referral Pipelines (Relationship)
**Companies to approach for referral partnerships:**
- Eastside Exterminators (Bothell) — they do insulation as secondary service; if overloaded, they could refer to Evrnew
- Other pest control companies in Everett, Bellingham area who don't do insulation
**Play:** Offer a referral fee or reciprocal referral arrangement.

### Priority 5: Build the Energy Rebate Lead Machine
**Why:** Underutilized by all competitors. Washington utilities (Snohomish County PUD, PSE, Puget Sound Energy) offer significant rebates for insulation upgrades.  
**Play:** Create landing pages specifically for "Washington insulation rebate," "Snohomish PUD insulation rebate," etc. These are informational search terms with buying intent and very low PPC competition.
